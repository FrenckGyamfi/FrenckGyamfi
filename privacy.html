Who is responsible

DataiTECH is the controller for the personal data described in this statement, as defined in the General Data Protection Regulation (AVG/GDPR).

What we collect

  • When you email us: your name, email address, organisation and anything you choose to include in your message.
  • When you visit the website: our web server processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which is needed to deliver the page securely.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts are hosted on our own server, so your visit is not shared with third parties such as Google.

Why we use it

  • To answer your question and follow up on a possible collaboration (legitimate interest, or steps before entering into a contract).
  • To keep the website secure and available (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for automated decision-making.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your question and any resulting collaboration, and no longer than legally required or allowed. Server logs are kept for a limited period for security purposes.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run our email and website, under a data processing agreement, and with authorities when the law requires it.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to data portability. Email us at enquiries@dataitechbv.com to make a request. We respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
