[01] Challenges we solve

Recognise this?

The questions we hear from infrastructure managers, architects and CISOs. If one of them is on your agenda, we should talk.

Backup & cyber resilience

Can you prove you can recover from ransomware?

Auditors and boards now ask for evidence, not a backup policy. We design immutable, air-gapped copies and test the recovery, so you can show it works.

How we help → Compliance-driven resilience

DORA, NIS2 or the BIO on your desk?

Financial entities must comply with DORA since January 2025, NIS2 comes into Dutch law through the Cyberbeveiligingswet, and government works to the BIO. We turn those requirements into concrete resilience and recovery measures.

How we help → Virtualisation & hybrid cloud

VMware licensing forcing a rethink?

Since the Broadcom acquisition many organisations face new VMware licensing and higher costs. We map your options: right-size, move to VMware Cloud Foundation, or shift workloads to Hyper-V or the cloud.

How we help → Storage & infrastructure

Storage at end of support, or simply full?

Refresh, migrate or consolidate without disrupting the business, with capacity and performance sized on your real workload data.

How we help → Data, classification & AI

Do you know where your personal data lives?

BSN numbers, HR files and medical records end up spread across shares and backups. We find, classify and protect them under the AVG (GDPR).

How we help → Container & Kubernetes storage

Containers in production, but who protects the data?

Persistent storage and backup for OpenShift and Kubernetes are often an afterthought. We make stateful containers as safe as the rest of your estate.

[06] Questions

FAQ.

Three topics we explain in almost every first conversation. Something else on your mind? Ask us.

What is data classification, and why does it matter?

Classification means labelling data by what it is and how sensitive it is: for example personal data under the AVG (GDPR), business-critical records, archive material and duplicates.

Once data is classified you can protect what is sensitive, store cold data more cheaply, delete what you no longer need, and feed AI only the data it should see.

What is the 3-2-1 backup rule?

Keep at least 3 copies of your data, on 2 different types of storage, with 1 copy off-site. Modern cyber recovery adds an immutable copy that cannot be changed or deleted, so you can still recover after a ransomware attack.

Can you help with the VMware licensing changes?

Yes. We start with what you actually run and what it costs today, then compare the realistic options: right-sizing your VMware estate, moving to VMware Cloud Foundation, or shifting workloads to Microsoft Hyper-V or the cloud. You get a recommendation based on cost, risk and timing, not on a vendor's preference.
